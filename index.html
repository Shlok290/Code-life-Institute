Comprehensive Font Formatting Tags Demonstration

Required inline HTML text styling elements tested below:

<B> & <STRONG>

This text is normal, but this part is bold (b tag) and this part is strong (strong tag) for importance.

<I> & <EM>

We can use italics for titles or foreign words (i tag) and emphasis for stressed emphasis (em tag).

<U> & <INS>

To highlight specific lines, we use underlined text (u tag) or inserted newly added text (ins tag).

<MARK> & <SMALL>

You can highlight important keywords with yellow backgrounds, or display fine print using the small tag.

<DEL> & <S>

Outdated pricing or removed items are shown with deleted strike-through text or strikethrough (s tag).

<SUP> & <SUB> & <CODE>

Mathematical formulas like E=mc2, chemical formulas like H2O, and inline snippet console.log('Test');.
